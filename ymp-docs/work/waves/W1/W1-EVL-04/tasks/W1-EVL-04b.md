# W1-EVL-04b — Message interventions separate appearance from causal use

## Outcome

Repeated matched-budget interventions determine whether selected participant messages change later
behavior and improve accepted outcome, calibration, recovery, or cost beyond message absence,
neutral text, shuffled text, and direct delivery of the same underlying evidence.

## Scope

### In

- Preregistered episodes and fresh-run variants for original message, no message, neutral payload,
  cross-task or cross-sender shuffle, direct evidence delivery, delay, false finding, and
  participant removal.
- Positive signaling, positive listening, task value, complementarity, revision quality, preserved
  dissent, adaptive organization, communication cost, and recovery measures.
- Published summaries, external actions, artifact ancestry, and verifier evidence only.

### Out

- Private chain-of-thought collection, consciousness claims, rewarding influence, changing the
  measured run's allocation policy, and treating natural language fluency as an outcome.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Every analyzed episode has frozen treatment assignment, equal declared budget, repeated
  stochastic observations, and a predeclared receiver behavior or task outcome.
- [ ] Signaling, listening, and task value are reported separately; temporal order, citation, or
  agreement alone is never labelled causal.
- [ ] Direct delivery of the same evidence is compared with participant delivery so that context
  duplication is not misreported as separate-participant value.
- [ ] An intervention with no receiver change or no task value yields a negative communication
  result even when the original transcript appears coherent or persuasive.
- [ ] Missing randomization, unequal budget, post-hoc episode selection, or unavailable treatment
  records excludes the causal claim through the analysis check.

## Current state

The intervention families are described in design documents, but no executable study harness,
episode sample, treatment assignment, or result exists.

## Next action

After the primary arms produce valid traces, freeze the episode sample before running any
communication intervention.

## Guardrails

- Communication measures remain offline and never affect candidate acceptance or participant
  eligibility.
- A leadership-like pattern requires unprompted formation, causal influence, outcome value, and
  transfer or re-formation after removal; centrality alone is insufficient.
- Results remain scoped to the tested tasks, groups, runtime profiles, and intervention design.
